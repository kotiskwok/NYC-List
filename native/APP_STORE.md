## App Information
- **Name:** Spotted in: Places To Go
- **Subtitle** (30 max): `Save spots from posts you love`  (no other apps' names: Apple rejects them in the subtitle and keywords under guideline 2.3.7)
- **Category:** Primary **Food & Drink**, Secondary **Travel**
- **Content rights:** **Yes**, it contains, shows or accesses third-party content, and **Yes**, you have the rights to it: place photos, hours and map data come from Google Maps Platform and Apple Maps under their API licenses, and posts are read only when the user shares a link.
- **Age rating:** the app has user-generated content (reviews, list notes, public maps), so answer **User-Generated Content: Yes** and **None** for everything else (violence, mature themes, gambling, etc.). Unrestricted web access: No. That usually lands at **12+** or **13+** in Apple's newer ratings.
- **License Agreement:** keep Apple's Standard EULA; our own terms are at https://nyc-lovat.vercel.app/terms.html (linked in the app at sign-up and in Your Account).

> SAVE IT IN ONE TAP

> WHERE ARE WE GOING TONIGHT?
> Swipe through your saved spots like a deck of cards, or describe a vibe ("somewhere cozy for a rainy day") and Spotted In picks from your own list. Walk near a spot you saved and it gives you a little nudge.

> PLAN A DAY, PLAN WITH FRIENDS

>
> RATE WHAT YOU TRY
> Leave stars, a comment and a photo, see your friends' Friend Score, and share a public map of your favorites with anyone.
>
> Your spots sync across your devices. No ads, no tracking.

## App Privacy (Data types)
Tracking: **No**.

Data collected. For every type: used for **App Functionality** only, **linked to the user**, **not used for tracking**.
| Category | Type | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Contact Info | Email Address | Sign-in |
| Contact Info | Name | Shown on shared lists and reviews |
| User Content | Photos or Videos | Spot, list and review photos; still frames read when adding from a video |
| User Content | Audio Data | Speech-to-text when adding from a video |
| User Content | Other User Content | Saved places, notes, lists, reviews, public map |
| Location | Precise Location | Home spot (optional), and the map area sent to improve place suggestions |
| Identifiers | User ID | The account behind your data |

## App Review Information
- **Sign-in required:** Yes. Demo account: make one in the app (e.g. `appreview.spottedin@gmail.com`), save 3–4 spots and make one list, then enter it here.
- **Contact:** Kotis Kwok · your phone · kwokshulum@gmail.com
- **Notes:**
> Spotted In is a personal map of places to try, saved from social media posts. Sign in with the demo account above.
> • Map tab: tap a pin for the place card (hours, nearest station, What To Order, Get There).
> • + button: paste a TikTok, Instagram, Threads or Google Maps link; AI fills in the spot.
> • Share extension: in Safari or TikTok tap Share → Spotted In; the link is filled in next time the app opens.
> • Lists tab: shared trip lists, private and invite-only.
> • User-generated content: reviews on place cards are visible to other users who saved the same place. Each review has Report and Block; reported reviews are hidden from the reporter at once and from everyone at 3 reports; we act on reports within 24 hours. Users agree to the Terms of Use (https://nyc-lovat.vercel.app/terms.html) when creating an account.
> • AI: the first time an AI feature is used, the app asks permission before sending anything to Anthropic (Claude) or OpenAI; this is also described in the Privacy Policy.
> • Account deletion: tap the profile photo on the Map tab → Your Account → Delete My Account (tap twice to confirm).
> Location is used only while the app is open.
