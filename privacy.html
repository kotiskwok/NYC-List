My NYC List

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

The short version: My NYC List keeps the places you save so they sync to your devices and to the lists you share. It doesn't show ads, doesn't track you across other apps or websites, and never sells your information.

What the app stores

  • Your account: your email address, an encrypted password and the name you choose.
  • Your map: the places you save (name, address, map pin, neighborhood, category, price, notes and the link you saved it from), your categories, your day plan and, if you set it, your Home spot.
  • Lists you make or join: the spots on them, who added each one and the names of the people on the list.

Your location

When you tap My Location, your phone's location is used to show where you are and to estimate travel times. It stays on your device and is never saved to our servers. The only location stored is a Home spot, if you choose to set one.

Services that help run the app

  • Supabase stores your account and data.
  • Vercel hosts the website and the app's server functions.
  • Anthropic (Claude) reads a link, post caption or place name you choose to fill in a spot.
  • OpenAI turns speech into text when you add a spot from a video. The audio is cut from the video on your phone first; only the sound and a few still frames are sent.
  • Google Maps Platform and OpenStreetMap (Photon and Nominatim) suggest places and find addresses from what you type.

These services only get what's needed for that one task, and they're used only when you use the feature.

Sharing

Nothing is public. A list is seen only by people who join it through your invite link. If you choose to share your map with a list, those people can see your saved places (not your Home spot) but can't change them. You can remove people, make a new invite link or stop sharing at any time.

Deleting your data

In the app, tap the spot counter on the Map tab, then Delete My Account. This permanently deletes your account, your map and the lists you made, and removes you from lists you joined. You can also delete single spots or lists whenever you like.

Children

My NYC List isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.
