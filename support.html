How do I save a spot from TikTok or Instagram?

In TikTok, Instagram, Threads or Google Maps, tap Share, then NYC List. The next time you open the app, it reads the post and fills in the spot for you to check. You can also paste a link into Add A Spot (the + button).

How do I plan a trip with a friend?

Open the Lists tab, tap + New List, add spots, then tap Share & Invite. Your friend opens the link, makes an account and can add and sort spots with you.

My location isn't showing

Go to iPhone Settings → NYC List → Location and choose While Using The App. You can also tap the map where you are.

How do I delete my account?

Tap the spot counter at the top of the Map tab, then Delete My Account and tap again to confirm. This deletes your account and everything in it.
